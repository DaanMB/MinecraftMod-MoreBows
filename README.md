<H1>MoreBows</H1>
<H2>A minecraft mod that adds 5 more bows</H2>

<img width="1920" height="1006" alt="2026-08-26_11 55 41" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c3e62a94-bd4b-400b-9393-66334a2ef985" />

<H3>This mod add the following bows</H3>
<p>1. The Pale Bow: a bow that shoot out arrows with 2x the velocaty, obtainable in a custom structure in the pale garden. <br>
2. The Basalt Bow: a bow that gives the arrows it shoots fire partacles, obtainable in bastion chests.<br>
3. The Spider Bow: a bow that spaws cobwebs on the block it lands on, obtainable in mineshaft chests.<br>
4. The Guardian Bow: a bow that draws 2x as fast, obtainable by killing a Elder guardian. <br>
5. The Super Bow: a bow that has all of the functionalities from the bows above, obtainable by smelting the other bows and putting the 4 orbs you get when the bows are smelted together with a vanilla bow in a crafting table.</p>
